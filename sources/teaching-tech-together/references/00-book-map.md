## The book in one paragraph

Grassroots groups teaching programming and other technical skills outside classrooms are usually run by people teaching themselves to teach. A few evidence-based facts about how people learn, how to design and deliver lessons, and how to grow a community of practice make those teachers much more effective. The book has four sections (§1 intro): how people learn (Ch 2–5, 7), how to design lessons that work (Ch 6, 12), how to deliver them (Ch 8–11), and how to build a community around teaching (Ch 13–15). The book follows its own advice: short, actionable ideas first, exercises throughout, and its own lesson design published as Appendix M.
