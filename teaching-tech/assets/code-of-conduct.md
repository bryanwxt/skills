# Code of conduct (template)

Adapted from the template in *Teaching Tech Together* (CC BY 4.0), which builds on the Ada Initiative / Geek Feminism Wiki. Fill in the bracketed parts. A code only works with a named contact and a real enforcement procedure.

**Our commitment.** We are dedicated to providing a harassment-free learning experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or level of experience.

**Unacceptable behaviour** includes: offensive comments; sexual images in public spaces; deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following; harassing photography or recording; sustained disruption of teaching or discussion; inappropriate physical contact; unwelcome sexual attention.

**Expected behaviour.** Be kind to others. Don't insult or put down other participants. Behave professionally. Remember that jokes that are sexist, racist, or otherwise exclusionary are not appropriate here.

**Enforcement.** Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behaviour must do so immediately. Organizers may take any action they judge appropriate, including warning the person, requiring an apology, or asking them to leave, at the instructors' discretion.

**Reporting.** Contact [NAME] at [EMAIL] or, during in-person events, [PHONE/TEXT] or any instructor. Reports are handled confidentially.

Thank you for helping make this a welcoming, friendly space for everyone.

---

**Instructor enforcement notes**
- Match the response to severity and intent: warning → apology → removal.
- Act in front of witnesses where possible.
- If someone is removed, tell the group briefly why.
- Tell the host or organizer as soon as possible and record what happened.
