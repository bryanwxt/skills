# Teamwork rubric (individual within a team)

Rate each item **Yes / Iffy / No / N/A**. Adapted from *Teaching Tech Together* (CC BY 4.0). Useful for group projects, pairing, and peer assessment.

**Communication**
- Listens without interrupting
- Checks understanding of what others said
- States ideas clearly and concisely
- Gives reasons for ideas
- Wins support from others

**Decision making**
- Looks at problems from several angles
- Uses logic to solve problems
- Proposes solutions based on facts, not gut feel
- Invites new ideas from others
- Generates new ideas
- Accepts change

**Collaboration**
- Raises issues the team needs to resolve
- Encourages differing ideas and opinions
- Works toward solutions acceptable to all
- Shares credit
- Encourages everyone to participate
- Accepts criticism openly
- Cooperates

**Self-management**
- Tracks progress toward goals
- Prioritizes getting results
- Sets priorities for work sessions
- Encourages contrary views
- Stays focused in meetings
- Uses meeting time efficiently
- Suggests ways forward
