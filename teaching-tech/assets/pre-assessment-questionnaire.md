# Pre-workshop questionnaire (template)

Pattern: one question on general experience, one on the specific tool, two concrete tasks of increasing difficulty with three-level answers, one open question on motivation. Ask about **tasks**, not self-ratings (people misjudge their own level), keep the tone gentle so novices aren't scared off, and follow up with people who don't respond.

**Intro text:** "This helps us pitch the workshop at the right level. There are no wrong answers — beginners are very welcome."

1. **How much programming have you done in general?**
   - None
   - A few lines now and then
   - Programs a couple of pages long for my own use
   - Larger software that I or others maintain

2. **How much have you used <TOOL/LANGUAGE>?**
   - None
   - A few lines now and then
   - Programs a couple of pages long for my own use
   - Larger software that I or others maintain

3. **How easily could you <SIMPLE CONCRETE TASK, e.g. find the largest number in a list> using <TOOL>?**
   - I wouldn't know where to start
   - I could struggle through with trial and error and lots of searching
   - I could do it quickly with little or no help

4. **How easily could you <HARDER CONCRETE TASK> using <TOOL>?**
   - (same three options)

5. **Why do you want to take this workshop? What would make it worthwhile for you?** (free text)

Optional:
6. Which operating system will you bring? (Windows / macOS / Linux / Chromebook / not sure)
7. Is there anything we can do to make the workshop more accessible for you? (free text)
